# Migration Plan

## Stage 0
Freeze new research campaigns.

## Stage 1
Introduce `InstrumentIdentity` and identity schema.

## Stage 2
Introduce `DatasetManifest` and provenance registry.

## Stage 3
Normalize providers and loaders.

## Stage 4
Introduce immutable `ExperimentManifest` and `CampaignManifest`.

## Stage 5
Make research engine consume manifests only.

## Stage 6
Centralize versioned validation and cost policies.

## Stage 7
Add behavioral evidence clustering.

## Stage 8
Formalize promotion state machine.

## Stage 9
Hard-separate research from execution.

## Stage 10
Migrate historical artifacts and quarantine unverifiable ones.

## Stage 11
Implement long-history capability or explicit provider limitation.

## Stage 12
Run clean bounded campaign on verified datasets only.
